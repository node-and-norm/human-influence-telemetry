## What changes in the evidence available

### 1. The chronology needs three separate events

The expanded documentary account distinguishes deliberation and the recorded decision on 16 August, announcement on 17 August, and later implementation. The original plan defines 17 August as its event day. A future assessment using these minutes must state its decision and observation windows explicitly. The prior announcement proposition and SR-01 wording can retain 17 August; they should not be relabeled as the date on which the Board first decided.

### 2. Evidence about access is more specific, but sufficiency remains a judgment

The original six-source packet supported a seasonal advice route. This supplement adds meeting-specific records of circulated material, pauses for consideration, reported information and advice. A later account should acknowledge those observations. It would be incomplete to summarize the expanded sources as showing only the existence of a formal route.

[Counsel in specification section 6.1](../SPECIFICATION.md#61-counsel) asks about actual, timely access to relevant underlying evidence sufficient to inform judgment. Reassessment must distinguish the Board from the Chief Regulator, underlying material from summaries, circulation from actual retrieval, and the earlier appeals decisions from the final reversal. Missing supporting papers do not establish absence of every form of access. Conversely, an identified briefing or paper does not by itself settle the relevance and sufficiency of its contents. No replacement category is assigned here.

### 3. Deliberation and authority now have additional recorded evidence

The minutes describe alternatives, questions, disagreement, successive resolutions and delegation. These are closer records of the decision process than an announcement alone. They remain edited institutional accounts with redactions. They do not establish complete deliberation, independence from political influence, normal-period performance or person-level remedy. This is a reason to review the rationales against an expanded packet, not to assume every finding improves.

### 4. The old temporal analysis must not be exported unchanged

[S05](../research/strengthening/solo-002-extension/report.md#s05-substitute-16-august-with-no-follow-up) tested a substituted 16 August boundary using the original sources. It expressly lacked a new source search. Its statement that the selected record did not establish the earlier exercise remains a description of that old packet. The minutes now supply earlier decision evidence, so the old conclusion cannot be carried into an expanded-source assessment without reassessment. Preserve the original S05 file and its unresolved interpretation.

### 5. Remaining limits are specific

The full reports, exam-board letters, legal advice and redacted discussions were not obtained. Neither individual access logs nor independently verified learner outcomes are available from this inspection. The additional documents share institutional provenance; more files do not equal more independent confirmations. Personal author source review, source-support adjudication and whole-draft approval remain pending.
