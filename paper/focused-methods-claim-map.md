## What manuscript, preprint and archive mean here

A manuscript is the working paper text. It may remain private or available as a repository draft while revision and review continue. Here, the current text is an unapproved methods/resource manuscript already included in the v0.6.7 software/research-artifact archive.

A preprint is a publicly shared version of a research paper before formal peer-reviewed publication. For HIT, posting one would require approval of the exact paper and the chosen publication route. This map does not create a preprint. The existing software DOI, 10.5281/zenodo.23252877, identifies the v0.6.7 repository archive; it supplies neither a separate preprint-publication decision nor peer-review acceptance. A later preprint, journal submission or revision of an existing paper needs its own scope, exact text and author approval. Do not invent a new DOI or reassign the software DOI to a paper.

## What a completed check must record

Use a separate future review record with these fields for each retained proposition:

- Claim ID, proposed sentence/caption, proposed section and exact draft revision.
- Supporting artifact revision or primary-source URL, version/date and precise page, section, paragraph or record key.
- Date actually accessed, extent actually read, and any access or preservation limitation.
- Supporting passage or concise paraphrase with locator, contrary material, reasoning and remaining uncertainty.
- Mechanical check performed, environment and observed result, kept separate from source interpretation.
- Responsible-author source-review disposition, reviewer and date; never prefill an attestation.
- Exact-draft wording decision and any omission or narrowing. An accepted description, source check, substantive judgment and whole-draft approval are separate fields.

Unresolved evidence may remain unresolved in an accurately bounded paper. A check is not completed merely because a link resolves, a script passes, a model agrees or a later-draft description was previously accepted.
