# Novelty Audit

## Candidate contribution

HIT combines seven documentary dimensions, explicit `0/1/2/IE` finding rules, split telemetry integrity, complete-record executable conformance, and a claim-gated research publication workflow. The repository demonstrates that combination as an open artifact.

## Claims currently permitted

The paper may describe the implemented combination and identify differences from reviewed adjacent systems after source-level comparison. It may claim no first, unique, or state-of-the-art status.

## Open tests

1. Complete a structured search across human oversight, meaningful human control, algorithmic accountability, contestability, assurance cases, and documentary audit methods.
2. Compare operational units, evidence thresholds, missing-evidence treatment, authority tests, repair, reform, and reproducibility controls.
3. Record near matches and revise the contribution statement when an earlier artifact contains the same combination.
4. Obtain external methodological review before submission when feasible.

Novelty remains provisional until these tests close.
