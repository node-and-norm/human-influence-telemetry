# Solo evaluation of documentary analytical value

Design HIT-SOLO-001, 2026-10-03 America/New_York. This prospectively replaces the six-reviewer usefulness pilot as the primary proposed evaluation path. No participant results or solo case-study findings are asserted. The earlier participant design remains a deferred extension. This is a research-design amendment following the author's instruction to make the program feasible for a solo independent researcher; it does not change the normative contract, historical results, maturity rules, or HIT-IRP-HIT040-002.

## Research decision

The primary question is: under explicitly bounded documentary conditions, what distinctions about practical human authority does HIT make reproducible and inspectable, what evidence supports those distinctions, and when do its conclusions remain unresolved?

The unit of evaluation is a claim and its evidentiary chain within a bounded institutional process. A solo study can supply worked analytical results, counterexamples, source-grounded applications, and reproducible software behavior. It cannot estimate variation between independent human raters, user time savings, adoption, or causal harm reduction. Those are separate future questions. Completion of this study must not change H3, H5, H8, H9, or maturity status by implication.

## Evidence strategy

Use three complementary forms of evidence, retaining disagreements:

1. Documentary application: source passages, actor/time boundaries, alternative interpretations, and explicitly authored judgments.
2. Analytical comparison: compare the same propositions under HIT and a capable structured documentary review, including ties and losses.
3. Controlled perturbation: test whether specific evidence changes alter only the conclusions they should affect, and whether irrelevant presentation changes leave judgments stable.

These forms test different failure mechanisms. They do not become independent replication merely by agreeing. Source independence must be evaluated proposition by proposition: three reports derived from one inquiry remain one evidentiary lineage for that proposition. Court, regulator, institutional, and technical records may also share upstream sources. Record their dependencies.

## Claim and falsifier register

| Claim sought | Evidence that could support it | Falsifier or limit |
|---|---|---|
| The representation preserves specified distinctions that a binary presence label loses | Explicit counterexample pairs and deterministic reproduction | The distinction cannot be represented, or the comparison silently adds evidence |
| An author can produce an inspectable documentary assessment under the current contract | Full source-to-proposition-to-finding chain with uncertainties and alternatives | A material finding has no admissible support or cannot be reconstructed from the packet |
| HIT adds analytical value over a structured review in named cases | A case-specific distinction, prevented inference, or actionable evidence request with a traceable reason | The simpler review produces the same information with equal or less burden; publish the tie or loss |
| A specified conclusion is sensitive to a material evidentiary change | Frozen before/after packets, explicit expectation, observed change | The conclusion persists after its decisive support is removed, without another adequate basis |
| A specified conclusion is stable under irrelevant changes | Order, formatting, and faithful paraphrase tests with retained outputs | Presentation changes alter the conclusion; report the instability |
| A finding has a stated evidentiary boundary | Alternative states of the world compatible with the available record are documented | A determinate conclusion is claimed despite unresolved alternatives that change its category |

No table row promises success. A negative result can require a narrower contribution, a handbook amendment, or a separately governed contract proposal.

## Stage 1: make the analytical contribution precise

Define common tasks before choosing favorable examples: identify who can intervene; distinguish nominal authority from usable intervention; distinguish absent capacity from unobserved capacity; attribute a change to the correct actor and period; and state what evidence could resolve an uncertainty.

One existing constructive example is U01 versus U02 in utility-cases.json: the declared binary comparator returns present for both, while encoded HIT Command findings are 2 and 1. This establishes information loss in that particular binary representation. It does not establish superiority over all alternatives or successful reading of natural documents.

The stronger comparator is a structured evidence table containing actor, period, proposition, source locator, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and next evidence request. Use identical packets and common questions. Write the comparator instructions before analysis and keep both outputs. Allow the comparator to identify every distinction it can. If HIT simply supplies standardized names and machine checking for equivalent content, report that narrower value.

## Stage 2: small public-document case series

Start with a feasibility inventory capped at six candidate packets, and aim for three completed analytical applications with differing documentary conditions. This is a workload and variation target, not a statistical sample-size justification. Eligibility requires a bounded process, an identifiable AI-mediated role, inspectable primary-source material, usable locators, disclosed source dependencies, and lawful citation or redistribution. Include candidates that challenge the framework and retain an exclusion log. Do not select by the HIT finding a case is expected to receive.

Existing historical applications are development material because the author and assistant have seen their findings. They may inform the feasibility inventory but cannot become unseen test cases. Record prior familiarity and model exposure. A later packet can be reserved before interpretation; call it reserved development material unless genuine separation from design and prior exposure can be established. A model's possible training exposure cannot be ruled out.

This design assigns no cases to the protected v0.7.0 slots. Their human-only selection rule and scoring prohibition remain in force. Any exploratory applications under HIT-SOLO-001 need their own identifiers, manifest, explicit author/AI roles, and a source-boundary freeze before substantive assessment. Findings from this track cannot be imported as independent submissions. No human attestation may be generated on the author's behalf.

For each application publish: the question and actor/period boundary; source inventory and lineage; exact passages and translation limitations; a proposition ledger; alternatives and counterevidence; the finding route, including exercise versus capability; specific missing evidence; and a reasoned conclusion limited to the case. Archive only materials permitted for redistribution; otherwise retain identifiers, access dates, locators, and retrieval instructions.

The author adjudicates interpretations and records revisions. AI may retrieve, extract, propose objections, and check consistency. Preserve who proposed each judgment and who accepted it. A passage from an official record establishes what that record reports; it does not automatically establish institutional reality or all HIT dimensions.

## Stage 3: sensitivity and ablation

Before observing changed outputs, declare each transformation and expected relation. Remove a decisive source, add materially conflicting evidence, substitute a different actor or period, or expose shared source lineage. Distinguish removing evidence from removing the underlying institutional capacity: evidence removal will often require IE rather than 0. Additional evidence can legitimately revise a finding in either direction, so no universal numerical monotonicity rule is assumed.

For ablation, remove one representational feature at a time: actor attribution, period boundaries, the IE state, or source-dependence disclosure. Assess which common task becomes unanswerable, conflated, or less explicit. The richer representation is expected to retain more fields; that fact alone is not a usefulness result. Show a consequential inference or evidence request affected by the omission, or record no added value.

Also test order, formatting, and faithful paraphrase. Record exact original and transformed bytes. Software and model results remain technical evidence. Author reanalysis measures only within-author stability, with memory and expectation effects disclosed. A washout period cannot create assessor independence.

## Stage 4: adjudication and reporting

For every comparator outcome record added distinction, tie, or loss, with both outputs and a source-based explanation. Record author time separately as descriptive effort, including setup and clarification; do not claim comparative speed from sequential self-use because familiarity, learning, and order are confounded. Preserve unresolved disputes and mixed results. Do not report dimension counts as independent institutional observations.

Use a reflexive memo for prior expectations, case familiarity, rule changes, and reasons for rejecting counterarguments. Delay global contribution statements until all selected cases, including failures, have dispositions. Keep study decisions and outputs versioned. No automatic pass rate can substitute for the claim-specific reasoning.

## Stop rule and publication package

After the capped feasibility inventory, freeze the selected applications and analysis plan. Complete every selected application or publish its incompleteness and reason. Do not replace a case because it produces an inconvenient result. If three feasible applications are unavailable, report the smaller study with a narrower claim instead of asserting coverage. Any change after seeing findings is exploratory and receives a dated amendment.

The intended paper package contains: a construct argument and closest-method comparison; inspectable case applications; the analytical comparator results; sensitivity/ablation findings; source and AI provenance; code and reproducibility instructions; and a claim register with unresolved limitations. A solo methods paper can be evaluated on those materials while external usability, reliability, and outcome studies remain future work. Venue acceptance and broad practical usefulness cannot be promised.

## Methods grounding

Venable, Pries-Heje, and Baskerville's [FEDS](https://doi.org/10.1057/ejis.2014.36) distinguishes evaluation purpose and artificial versus naturalistic settings. Its abstract and evaluation-framework discussion support selecting evaluation episodes to match the artifact property and claim. This design uses that distinction; it does not treat artificial tests as naturalistic effectiveness evidence.

Flyvbjerg's [Five Misunderstandings About Case-Study Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1186) challenges blanket dismissal of case-study contributions and emphasizes context-dependent inquiry. The abstract was consulted here. It does not establish the adequacy of this particular sample or permit population estimates.

Hallgren's [reliability tutorial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3402032/), A Primer on IRR, distinguishes agreement among coders from construct validity. That distinction is why the solo track makes no replacement claim for the existing human replication program.

These are design rationales from a targeted methods review, not a complete methodology review or independent endorsement. This protocol itself is AI-assisted and remains open to substantive author revision.
