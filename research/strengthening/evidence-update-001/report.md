That observation does not establish equivalence, comparative usefulness or HIT's necessity.

## Item-level interpretation

In the unchanged control, both responses retained all eight substantive assessments. For source withdrawal, both removed affirmative support for panel existence without treating withdrawal as proof that no panel existed. For the corrected date, both revised the direction to 18 August 2040 while retaining its actor and eligibility limits. For the contradictory account, both marked actual issuance as conflicted while retaining the fact that the original report was published. For new receipts, both limited the positive receipt statement to A01, A02, A03, A04 and A05, leaving other recipients and consequential harm unresolved.

The coordinating assistant read both complete responses and found these distinctions in their prose. This is an AI-assisted content inspection, not a prespecified human semantic rating or author acceptance. The scorer verifies enums and two literal content patterns; it cannot establish that every statement or rationale is adequate. ## What this contributes—and what it does not

The reusable contribution is a frozen task, equal-information comparison packets, retained first responses and an offline scoring route. It makes future changes and disagreements inspectable. It supplies no new historical evidence about Ofqual, no new HIT category findings and no independent human evidence.

The task is small and highly cued: the update notices state their changes explicitly, the permitted labels are defined, and both representations supply all relationships. Full agreement may reflect an undemanding task rather than robust assessment ability. The observed equality gives no basis for preferring the graph over the table. It does not establish real-world error detection, reduced effort, improved decisions or operational scalability. The author's earlier Ofqual comparison decision remains open; this different synthetic task does not adjudicate it.

The next consequential evaluation should test a specified user's evidence-maintenance task with equivalent source access and accountable review of substantive answers. It should retain failures and measure any claimed effort or decision benefit. This rehearsal can inform that design; it does not authorize participant recruitment or replace the current-contract replication and independent implementation requirements. More runs of this easy task would not, by themselves, resolve those questions.
