## Findings

Its Reform rationale records unobserved exercise, while the current 0.4.0 rule distinguishes absent authority, formal authority, operative change, and unresolved evidence. Historical findings must remain version-bound. A new application needs applicable primary-source passages, actor attribution, search coverage, and a recorded decision among those states. CA-02 and CA-03 constrain the demonstrated result. Synthetic inputs establish how code handles declared facts. The existing two-scorer exercise establishes agreement on one packet under 0.1.0. Statistical analyses must respect dependence among dimensions within a case; item count must not inflate the apparent case sample.

CA-04 limits the integrity badge. A passing audit establishes declared control behavior over mapped claims. The truth of source propositions and the completeness of manuscript mapping require separate scrutiny. A model-generated support judgment must not be recorded as human support review.

A future outcome protocol should predeclare nonoverlapping predictors and outcomes, temporal ordering, sector and severity confounders, and the role of evidence availability. H5 remains untested.

## Open construct questions

1. Do assessors distinguish missing documents from missing practical control when institutions have different recordkeeping resources?
2. Can staged or retrospective reasons appear to establish Judgment? Require chronology, corroboration, and a documented source-dependence analysis.
3. Do Counsel and Judgment, or Command and Correction, produce distinguishable findings in real packets? Use counterexamples with differing dimensions; no single total score is proposed.
4. How much evidence establishes a dominant period pattern? Test sensitivity to sampling rules and exceptional events without silently changing the unit of analysis.
5. Does the distinction between exercised power and tested capability matter to users? Report both routes and test their interpretations.

These questions are recorded limitations and test targets. They are not adjudicated defects in the preserved contract.
