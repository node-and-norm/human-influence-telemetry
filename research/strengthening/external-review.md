## Reviewer instructions

Read the selected material without assuming the author's findings are correct. Record what you inspected and what you could not inspect. Distinguish a source error, a rule ambiguity, a disagreement with the normative choice, and an implementation defect. Use [review-response.template.json](review-response.template.json) or equivalent prose with the same fields. Cite the smallest reproducible passage. State what evidence would change your view.

## Author disposition

Preserve the original response; append accepted, rejected-with-reason, or unresolved disposition separately. Link any resulting change to the response and tests. Never rewrite a reviewer response to align with the final paper. Maintain a public status of requested, received, permission-pending, or published without exposing private contact details. Review completion cannot be inferred from an invitation or acknowledgment.
