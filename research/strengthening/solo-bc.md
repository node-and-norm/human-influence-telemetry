# Announced change and recommended reform: documentary limits

The selected records leave implementation unresolved. These two partial applications examine what the records support and what further evidence an assessor would need. Codex prepared the interpretations under the [selection freeze](solo-series.md); the author has not adjudicated them. Neither application assigns a complete HIT score.

## B: Ofqual and OCR, 17 August 2020

### Observations and source lineage

In [Ofqual's statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/statement-from-roger-taylor-chair-ofqual), the paragraphs beginning “After reflection” and “We have therefore decided” announce the grading change. The final paragraph describes implementation as planned. The [OCR announcement](https://www.ocr.org.uk/news/switch-to-centre-assessment-grades-for-as-a-level-and-gcse-results/), dated 17 August 2020, repeats the decision, attributes it to Ofqual, and promises further implementation details. OCR's final paragraph leaves implications for Cambridge Technicals and Cambridge Nationals under review.

Accessed 2026-10-04 UTC. OCR supplies evidence of its own communication, but its account of Ofqual's decision depends on Ofqual. The two pages therefore do not provide two independent confirmations of completed implementation. No individual revised grade, delivery receipt, or later implementation record belongs to this packet.

|---|---|---|---|
| Who announced the change? | Attribute the regulatory decision to Ofqual and the downstream communication to OCR. | Preserve the actor boundary before considering Command or Reform. | Tie: the baseline preserves the same attribution. |
| What implementation is established? | The packet records an announced decision and implementation work. Request dated operational instructions and evidence that the new process ran. | Reform requires an explicit assessment object. Issuing a policy decision and changing an operational workflow demand different evidence. | Tie: HIT supplies category names without resolving the missing implementation evidence. |
| What remedy reached individuals? | Delivery remains unverified within this packet. Request corrected results, affected-person coverage, and records of unresolved consequences. | Repair requires attention to the remedy and its recipient. A changed process alone cannot establish that every relevant remedy reached its recipient. | Tie: both methods identify the same evidence request. |

### Alternative interpretation and limitation

A regulator can exercise authority by issuing a decision. Treating every announcement as mere aspiration would discard that act. Conversely, attributing downstream delivery to the announcement would exceed the packet. A future assessment must define whether its object is the regulatory act, the awarding body's workflow, or the student's remedy before applying a threshold.

Removing OCR's page would remove the awarding body's acknowledgement and qualification-specific uncertainty from the packet. It would leave Ofqual's announcement intact. This is a qualitative dependency argument; no automated source-removal experiment was run. Later evidence may establish implementation, but the frozen boundary excludes it. The application cannot establish that implementation failed.

## C: Royal Commission recommendations 17.1 and 17.2

### Observations and source lineage

The [official report page](https://robodebt.royalcommission.gov.au/publications/report), under Chapter 17, recommends that the Commonwealth consider a consistent legislative framework for automation. Recommendation 17.1 also addresses review paths, public explanations, and scrutiny of business rules and algorithms. Recommendation 17.2 proposes consideration of an auditing body or expanded existing body. The page records initial publication on 7 July 2023 and a corrected edition on 11 July.

Accessed 2026-10-04 UTC. The packet contains the current recommendation text, without a verified publication-day snapshot. The two recommendations share one inquiry lineage. No implementation response, legislation, operational audit, or recipient remedy record was inspected within this application.

### Matched comparison

| Common question | Structured documentary review | HIT interpretation | Proposed outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| What action is sought? | Record the proposed governance changes with their recommendation status. | Map the proposal to relevant Correction and Reform questions while keeping the recommendation distinct from an operative mechanism. | Tie. |
| Who would implement it? | Separate the Commission's recommendation from Commonwealth implementation; request an accountable implementing body and adopted plan. | Actor attribution prevents transferring the Commission's conduct to an implementing agency. | Tie. |
| What operative reform or delivered remedy is established? | This packet does not resolve either question. Request implementation instruments, operational records, and any claimed remedy's delivery evidence. | The recommendation alone does not satisfy operative-change or delivered-remedy predicates. Missing evidence does not establish absent capacity. | Tie. |

### Alternative interpretation and limitation

Recommendations may influence later institutional action. This application has no evidence to estimate that influence. The packet also cannot determine whether relevant review routes already existed. Reading a proposed improvement as proof of total prior absence would introduce an unsupported inference.

Removing the recommendation text would remove this packet's evidence of the proposal. It would establish nothing about the underlying institution's capacity. This is qualitative reasoning, not a measured sensitivity result. The application tests a documentary boundary and supplies no assessment of the whole Robodebt scheme.
