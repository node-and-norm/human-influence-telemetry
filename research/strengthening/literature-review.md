## Contribution that survives this comparison

HIT implements a versioned record connecting six practical-authority dimensions to evidence states, actor and time boundaries, explicit insufficient evidence, separate record/packet integrity, and executable conformance. This combination is observable in the repository. Its incremental benefit over a simpler review remains a testable proposition. No first, unique, superior, or comprehensive status follows from this targeted review.

| Correction | Contestability and revision | Does a channel work for affected people with unequal access? |
| Repair | Remediation to people | Are remedy delivery and adequacy distinguishable? |
| Reform | Change to institutional decision architecture | Which actor, period, and level of architecture does a change support? |

This crosswalk records conceptual adjacency, not a historical derivation of HIT or validated equivalence. It does not establish that six dimensions are necessary or sufficient. A counterexample in which two dimensions diverge is useful evidence about discriminability; a missing construct is a reason to reconsider scope through versioned governance.
