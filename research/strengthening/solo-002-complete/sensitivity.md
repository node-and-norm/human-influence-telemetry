# Source-subset reanalysis

HIT-SOLO-002, 7 October 2026. Codex performed this qualitative reanalysis after the full-packet draft. The [plan](plan.md) predeclared the expected relations before the draft findings. Each condition below identifies the admissible source subset and records proposed answers under the same six question templates used by the [capable baseline](baseline-and-review.md).

This is executed within-assistant reasoning over source availability and attribution. It is not an automated extraction evaluation, a human reanalysis, an independent replication or a statistical experiment. The assistant retained knowledge of the full packet while considering each restricted condition. Expectation and memory effects are unavoidable here. The same assistant authored the expectations, applied the conditions and judged their relation; no separate evaluator or model request supplied these results. They inspect the stated argument's dependence on sources and do not estimate how reliably another analyst would discover the argument.

Source identifiers resolve through the [source ledger](source-ledger.md). No source text is changed. For condition S04 only, the assessed actor in the question template is changed to an individual teacher. That is a constructed attribution test, not a new historical claim. Each baseline and HIT column is assistant-authored and awaits author review.

## S04: substitute an individual teacher as the assessed authority

Admissible sources: all S00 sources. Only the target actor in the six question templates changes from Ofqual's governing authority to an unspecified individual teacher; the same event and follow-up remain. Expected relation: evidence about a regulator does not transfer that regulator's powers to a teacher.

| Common question | Proposed baseline answer | Proposed HIT answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: authority | The packet does not identify a teacher empowered to issue the regulator's direction. | The original authority claims do not support the substituted actor. Missing evidence is not a finding that teachers had no relevant powers. |
| Q2: access and reasoning | The Chair's reasons and Board advice are not that teacher's reasons or access record. | Counsel and Judgment cannot inherit the original actor-specific evidence. |
| Q3: change | The changed rule and grade issue remain historical reports; their existence does not establish teacher control of the systemic reversal. | Command, Correction and Reform claims need a separate actor-specific chain. No complete teacher assessment is fabricated. |
| Q4: harm and remedy | Affected candidates and institutional direction remain described, but the teacher's remedial ownership is unresolved. | Repair cannot transfer the regulator's operational direction to the teacher. |
| Q5: limits | No named teacher, delegation instrument, individual intervention or remedy record is in this packet. | The actor-reference problem remains substantive even if a JSON record can be made structurally valid. |
| Q6: next step | Request a named actor and applicable authority/evidence records before assessing that person's role. | The same request follows; do not label absence from this packet affirmative absence of capacity. |

Observed analytical change: the source propositions retain their original actors while the proposed substituted actor lacks support. Both methods reject the transfer. Proposed tie. This tests attribution within an already understood packet; it does not show how often independent assessors avoid attribution errors.

## Disposition

The assistant's recorded reanalysis follows the four tested expectations. No numerical accuracy rate is calculated, and no inference about comparative user performance follows. One source removal changed a material support claim; another preserved execution while weakening narrower attribution. The duplicate and actor conditions preserve distinctions that the capable baseline also makes.

The period-substitution, conflicting-source and faithful-formatting conditions declared in the plan remain unexecuted. No invented conflicting historical document was introduced. End-to-end automated document extraction, inter-rater reliability, user burden, adoption and institutional effects remain untested. Author adjudication of these qualitative outcomes is pending alongside the base assessment.
