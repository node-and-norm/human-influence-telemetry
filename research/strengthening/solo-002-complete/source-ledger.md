## Independence and temporal checks

OFQ-01 through OFQ-04 belong to one institutional reporting lineage. The Department for Education is a separate publisher involved in the same response; the packet cannot establish whether its implementation account was independently verified. OCR independently records its communication but relies on Ofqual for the regulatory decision. Their agreement is corroborative context with disclosed dependence, not multiple independent studies.

OFQ-02's accessible HTML is dated 20 July 2021. OFQ-03 is titled April 2021 but its accessible HTML is marked updated 11 April 2024; its current text cannot authenticate the wording or availability of the 2021 version. The substantive text was read as current institutional reporting about the earlier event. No earlier snapshot was recovered, and the scope or reason for the 2024 update was not established. This retrieval qualification does not silently change the frozen source boundary or automatically determine a finding.

DFE-01's accessible update postdates the follow-up boundary by a week. These publication and update dates are not dates on which the Board exercised authority. The assessment uses only statements about the declared event and follow-up. OFQ-04 does not establish deployment dates on its own.

The four EC-ISSUE propositions in the draft separate consequence, reconsideration, remedy and operative rule use under specification section 11. They draw on one DFE-01 paragraph and share its lineage. Splitting the reasoning does not create additional institutional observations or independent corroboration.

Later implementation evidence changes what this enlarged packet can establish. It does not make the original announcement-only dossier incorrect under its narrower boundary. Conversely, the expanded sources do not establish that every student received a satisfactory remedy, that all individual appeals were accessible, that any particular grade was accurate, or that HIT caused an institutional improvement.

## Material missing records and contrary possibilities

The packet lacks the specific pre-decision evidence bundle and access history for the 17 August decision; individual deliberation and voting records; candidate-linked before/after grade records; and a reconciliation from acknowledged harm to recipient-level remedy. It also lacks independent inspection of institutional retention and edit controls. The public reasons could be a selective retrospective account. Official implementation reports could omit exceptions.

The annual report's position on calculated-grade bias does not negate the Chair's acknowledgement of distress or settle whether an individual grade was wrong. An architecture change can therefore be evidenced without a finding on the original model's accuracy, fairness, or legality. Each proposed finding must preserve that distinction.

No external records request was sent. The search consisted of the plan's reconnaissance queries followed by inspection of all six frozen URLs and source-internal section searches for the declared questions. Missing evidence is a packet limitation; it is not affirmative evidence that the corresponding authority or record never existed.
