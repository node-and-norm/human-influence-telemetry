# E5 writing rules for this workbench

Prose must preserve the evidence when it changes the sentence. The author supplied Source Packet for E5 Grammar-to-Prose Learning Guide, Part 9 of 9, during preparation of the documentary series. This note applies that packet to HIT; it does not reproduce the packet or claim that the missing original materials have been recovered.

Review the argument before polishing words. Identify the reader's question, state each paragraph's proposition, and trace the evidence and reasoning that support it. Separate a source observation from an interpretation, an uncertainty, and a recommendation. Keep actor, period, assessment object, and source limits explicit.

At sentence level, check the subject, finite verb, agreement, clause boundaries, modifiers, and pronoun references. Put substantive actions in verbs when that clarifies responsibility. Preserve passive voice when the actor is unknown; an active rewrite must not invent an actor. Use causal language only when the evidence supports causation.

Cut repetition that adds no meaning, preserve necessary qualifications, and retain technical terms when they distinguish concepts. Vary sentence structure to follow the reasoning. Audit generic openings, promotional claims, mechanical lists, and habitual contrast constructions. These are contextual revision tests, not universal grammar prohibitions.

The supplied packet distinguishes recovered material, supported synthesis, derived examples, and unresolved source recovery. Preserve those labels when teaching or attributing E5. In particular, the paragraph sequence claim, evidence, mechanism, boundary, implication is a supported synthesis; it is not a mandatory template or recovered quotation.

Finish with an integrity pass: did revision change the actor, certainty, scope, technical meaning, or logical relationship? Automated prose checks can flag patterns for review. They cannot certify E5 quality or scientific support. Where the general writing-system skill imposes a stylistic absolute that conflicts with this supplied packet, follow the author's E5 packet.
