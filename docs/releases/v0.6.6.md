- Required-file and hash checks, safe-path checks, readiness failure modes, and 27 regression tests. A passing staging check remains distinct from audit or release readiness.
- Public implementation instructions, a ten-task catalog, a candidate submission schema and an unperformed template. No eligible clean-room audit is recorded.

Its 23 evidence claims, matched baseline and four qualitative source-subset reanalyses remain assistant proposals pending author adjudication. Eight record-boundary tests accompany the draft; they do not validate source truth.
