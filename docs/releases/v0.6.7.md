## What changed

Version 0.6.7 packages the research-development work merged in PRs [#35](https://github.com/node-and-norm/human-influence-telemetry/pull/35), [#36](https://github.com/node-and-norm/human-influence-telemetry/pull/36) and [#37](https://github.com/node-and-norm/human-influence-telemetry/pull/37). It also retains the simplified citation navigation from [#34](https://github.com/node-and-norm/human-influence-telemetry/pull/34). It is a nonbreaking software/research-artifact release. Packaging the working manuscript does not submit it as a paper or accept its scientific claims.

- Retained fourteen Jev advisory responses with source-scope limits and offline replay. They provide no independent human judgment.
- Recorded the author's bounded Ofqual decisions: five event-level substantive findings and limited status for both integrity components. Counsel and the overall documentary comparison remain unresolved.
- Added three qualitative period, conflict and presentation conditions, with their interpretations pending author review. The original assessment and earlier analyses remain intact.
- Added a current methods/resource manuscript and a sixteen-claim descriptive register. Responsible-author review and exact-draft publication approval remain pending.
- Added three source-bound figures: assessment architecture, Ofqual event/publication timing and qualification preservation. Each has text data, source bindings, a vector file and a preview.
- Froze an equivalent-information graph/table evidence-update task before collecting two first-attempt assistant responses. Both identified all four injected changes, with no scored difference. This small, highly cued synthetic task demonstrates no comparative advantage, human benefit or full-method equivalence.
- Added tracked-file/dependency verification, response-bound replay and figure reproduction checks. Software checks test specified properties; they do not establish source truth or scientific validity.
- Amended historical replay for three release-facing files. Exact copies of the former research summary, citation and Zenodo metadata remain bound to their original commit; current release metadata is checked separately. This permits a new release without rewriting experiment inputs.

The [evidence-update report](../../research/strengthening/evidence-update-001/report.md), [working manuscript](../../paper/development-manuscript.md) and [figure guide](../../figures/development-2026-10-08/README.md) provide the detailed record. The new record was prepared with AI assistance. Separate assistant contexts do not establish independent review or guaranteed isolation.
